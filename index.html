Wallscout Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Wallscout is an Android app published by LewisFab (Lewis Fabrication LLC). It sets your home and lock screen wallpapers using images from wallhaven.cc.

Information we collect

None. Wallscout has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not send any information to us.

Information stored on your device

Your settings, search terms, favourites and wallpaper history are stored only on your device. If you use the app's export feature, the backup file is saved wherever you choose. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include Wallscout's settings in your Google account backup, under Google's privacy policy.

Wallhaven

To find and download wallpapers, Wallscout connects directly to wallhaven.cc and sends your search terms and display preferences (such as image orientation). Like any website, Wallhaven can see your device's IP address when it receives these requests. Wallhaven's handling of that information is covered by its own policies at wallhaven.cc. Wallscout only requests images marked safe-for-work.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to download wallpapers and wait for a connection.
  • Set wallpaper: to change your home and lock screen wallpapers.
  • Notifications: to tell you if an automatic wallpaper change fails (optional).

Children

Wallscout is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
